📖 About

Pearls of Hope Foundation is a nonprofit that supports teenage mothers and vulnerable girls aged 13–17 through mentorship, education and emotional care. We believe every girl deserves a second chance and a future full of hope.

⸻

🎯 Mission

To empower young girls and teen moms to reclaim their future with confidence, purpose, and support.
